## Gaps and things to confirm

1. **The phone frame rate is not met on the proxy.** The 2D map reaches 30 fps at a CPU 2× slower than this PC, not at 4×. The remaining cost is deck.gl layer updates and MapLibre drawing. Next steps if the device is below 30: lower label density (`unit-labels`, `contact-labels`), drop text layers while the map moves, or merge layers. A real mid-range phone may be faster or slower than the 4× stand-in; measure with the README's console snippet (`WB_ANDROID_DEBUG=1` build).
2. **The APK was never built.** The Gradle project, manifest and `MainActivity.java` are unbuilt and untested; expect small fixes on first `assembleDebug`. Needs JDK 21 and the Android SDK.
3. **Dependencies (rule 7).** Capacitor 8.6.0 (MIT) was named by the phase prompt, so I added it without asking. Its CLI pulls 13 build-time packages under **BlueOak-1.0.0** and 2 under **Unlicense**, none on the allowlist; none ship in the app. Please confirm or drop `apps/android`. 8.6.0 was chosen because 8.6.1/8.6.2 are under two weeks old. Listed in `docs/versions.md`.
4. **R2 CORS** must allow the origin `https://localhost` for the hosted packs to download in the app (owner step); `allowMixedContent` is on for the LAN pack host.
5. **Suppression and strike can start on the same tick** when the strikers are far slower than needed to wait (`delay` 0): in operations 3 and 4 both phases started at the same ms. The strike still flies behind the missiles by distance, but a minimum 1-minute gap is a one-line change if you want a strict order.
6. The League AI planned no operation in 8 h (the Pact did four); not investigated (likely its picture of the Pact has fewer typed objectives). Acceptance needs only one side.
7. The AI does not re-plan a running operation, and the long-press is on the 2D map only (3D keeps the browser's own).
8. Old saves (version 5) are refused. `ForcesPanel` still renders every group (no virtualised list).
