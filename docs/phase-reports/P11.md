# P11 — Ground forces: phase report

Date: 11 Nov 2026. Status: **engine done locally; CI not yet run; the client part is thin** (see gaps). Checks ran on the working tree on Windows 11. I made no commits (CLAUDE.md rule 10); the owner committed checkpoints along the way.

Scope: the P11 prompt (doc 09). The design choices the docs left open are in **ADR-048 (Proposed)**.

## What was built

| Prompt item | Result |
|---|---|
| 1. Formations | A formation is a `system` with `deploy_as: formation`; its components are equipment lines. In the engine it is one group with one platform entity carrying `Formation`: equipment counts, personnel (crew × count), posture, morale, supply, fatigue. Platforms that fight on the ground have a `ground` stat block. Inventories, deployment and the setup builder place formations like other systems. |
| 2. Movement, FOBs | `wb_geo::path`: A* over the H3 cells of the finest pack, integer costs, the same route natively and in WASM. Speed = slowest equipment × posture × terrain factor (realism `ground.speed_factor`); water cannot be entered, marsh not by heavy equipment. An army base placed with `PlaceBase` is the FOB: it resupplies formations within 30 km once active, as preset air and naval bases do. |
| 3. Combat | Stage `ground`. Every 60 s formations within 3 km of an enemy fight (Lanchester-style, ±20% variation, terrain and posture protection, morale, supply, fatigue, night, EW and suppression factors). Tube artillery supports from range; rocket artillery fires real weapons through strikes and fire missions. Retreat, destruction, breakthrough. Strikes remove equipment by effective warhead, posture exposure and each line's protection. |
| 4. SOF and recon, ground tasks | Orders `move`, `attack`, `defend`, `hold`, `withdraw`, `recon`, `raid`, `designate` (plus `strike` for formations with weapons). Teams are hard to find (signature × posture size factor; coarse satellite imagery misses them), classify to type and count with their own sensors, sabotage a base component on a raid, and designate a contact (CEP × 0.3 for laser-capable weapons). |
| 5. Strike chain | `scenarios/golden/strike-chain.yaml` (G17): satellite report → drone upgrade → air strike, with a rocket battery as a parallel phase. |
| Also | Synthetic test terrain takes land patches with a class and an owner. Golden metrics `event_sequence`, `strength_lost`, `loss_ratio`, `equipment_left`. Ten new events, each to the formation's own side only. Fixtures: tank, rifle section, gun, rocket launcher, recon team, drone, their sensors and weapons, six formations. Event lines in the client's feed. State hash 7, save version 5, pack schema 7. |

## Decisions and deviations (please confirm; ADR-048)

1. **Formations are systems**, not a new content kind, and **one entity per formation**, not one per vehicle.
2. **Routes are found at tick time**, not when the order is validated (the order is still checked for a route so it can be refused with a reason). One order can then serve several formations and follow a moving contact.
3. **The attrition formula**: terrain protects (divides losses) only formations in a defence posture; an attacker's own losses do not depend on its terrain. Losses are carried exactly between steps, so the only randomness is the bounded variation the doc asks for.
4. **Realism values changed**: `defender_terrain_factor` was open 1.0, forest 1.5, urban 2.0, mountain 2.0 (placeholders). It is now forest 1.4, mountain 1.7, urban 2.2, so urban and mountain differ as doc 05 §3 describes ("strong defence bonus" against "defence bonus"). `lanchester_coefficient` 0.05 → 0.012 per step.
5. **A hit on a formation does not check how far the burst was from it.** The existing endgame decides hit or miss from the CEP roll alone; a formation is treated as an area target.
6. **Engine changes outside ground code:**
   - An ordered strike may release a CEP weapon on the side's estimate of a ground contact, without a Pk table entry for the class and without its own track. Before, catalogue bombs (no `pk_base`) could never be dropped on a classified ground contact.
   - A group heading home after a strike no longer raises `StrikeAborted` every second (it did when every round was fired).
   - A later resolved look updates a contact's count (it was fixed at the first resolved look).
7. **`Hold`** means "no retreat on morale"; a formation still gives way below 40% strength.
8. **Golden numbering**: G17 and G18 (G16 is the play-modes check of doc 12).

## Known gaps

- **Client.** Formations appear on the map with the existing ground symbols and their events read in plain English, and `details` carries a `formation` block (equipment, personnel, posture, strength, morale, supply). There are **no ground task buttons, no formation section on the unit card and no chevron frame** (doc 06 §3). A one-point "Move here" already works as a march; other air and sea tasks are refused with a reason. I did not run the app or take screenshots this phase, so the UI checklist of CLAUDE.md is not done. The Playwright suite was not run.
- **Captured ground** (doc 03 §3 says it arrives with P11) is not built: territory does not change when formations advance.
- **No catalogue content.** The ground fixtures exist; `content/` has no tanks, guns, infantry or formations yet (doc 03 names MBT-3 and RA-6), and no inventory lists them.
- **Enemy AI** does not task formations (P12).
- **Glide bombs fall short at maximum range**: the test bomb launched at its flyout range ran out of energy at 19 of 25 km, so the strike chain sets the strikers' doctrine to `close`, as G13 does. This is an existing flyout issue, found here, not fixed.
- **Rocket launchers are not rearmed in the field**; supply covers direct combat only.
- **Roads and rivers** are not modelled (doc 02 §5: later). Fatigue has no weather.
- **Reports**: equipment losses of formations are in the events but not in the losses table of the after-action report.
- **Multi-waypoint marches** are refused; a formation takes one destination.
- Tube artillery support needs a friendly formation in contact with the target; it does not fire on contacts alone (rocket artillery does, through strikes).
