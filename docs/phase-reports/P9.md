# P9 — Enemy AI v1, war setup and play modes: phase report

Date: 10 Nov 2026. Status: **done locally; CI not yet run** (checks ran on the working tree on 10 Nov 2026, Windows 11). Nothing is committed (CLAUDE.md rule 10).

Built in four checkpoints, each reviewed by the owner before the next: engine controllers and the umpire view; the setup builder and deploy-yourself; the browser client; the AI.

## What was built

| Area | Result |
|---|---|
| Controllers and modes (doc 12 §1, ADR-044) | Each side has a `controller` (human, AI) and a `difficulty`; `Sides.mode` is `vs_ai`, `command_both`, `hot_seat` or `watch`, derived from the controllers unless two humans choose hot-seat. Scenarios default every side to human, so G1–G14 are unchanged in their bands. State hash format 6. |
| Session (doc 12 §1) | `wb_sim::session`: active side, hot-seat settings, PIN hashes (salted BLAKE3, 4 digits), `umpire_enabled`, `umpire_used`. Lives beside the world in `Sim`, never in the snapshot; saved in its own section (save version 4). |
| Orders | A client order for an AI side is refused. An order naming another side's unit, base, contact, request or operation is refused with the same text an unknown id gets; the cause is kept as a typed `RejectKind` (ADR-046). |
| Umpire view | `Sim::umpire_view` only in Command both with the switch on; first use marks `umpire_used`, shown in the after-action report. SideView 4: header flag `UMPIRE` (side 255), owner `side` column on unit and weapon rows, no contacts. |
| WebAssembly API | Per-side `details`, `contact_details`, `approvals`, `operations`, `report`, `inventory`, `ground_track`, `deployment`, `take_events` (a cursor per side); `set_active_side`, `set_view_side`, `set_umpire`, `set_hot_seat`, `set_pin`, `check_pin`, `session`; `order` always uses the active side. `create_from_setup`, `nation_options`, `preview_setup`. |
| War setup builder (doc 03 §2, ADR-046) | `wb_sim::setup`: `WarSetup` → `Scenario`. Garrisoned from the inventories (basing rows first, then the base with most room; sites round capital, cities and bases; satellites in orbit; spare munitions shared by capacity); unplaced forces reported. Theatre North straits (League v Pact): 827 platforms and 118 systems, one row not placed (10 Vessmar `rw-5`: every suitable base full). |
| Deploy yourself | Human sides station their inventory pool at their own preset bases with `DeployForce`, then `FinishDeployment`; the engine holds the clock (`Sim::advance` runs no tick) until every deploying side has finished. AI sides garrison. |
| Client: setup and menu | The app opens on a menu (NEW WAR, QUICK START, LOAD WAR, MAP PACKS). Setup steps: Coalitions (A / B / — per nation, flags, forces, doctrine), Players (Human or AI, difficulty, same person or two players, hot-seat settings and PINs), Start (start mode, time, default ROE per side), Check (summary, unplaced forces, pack check with "start without the region pack"). |
| Client: play modes | Side toggle with flags and Tab; per-side UI slices (camera, selection, panels, alerts, logs, planner); each human side's event stream kept apart, a waiting side's alerts held; auto-slow from the active side only; territory re-tinted in place on a switch. Hot-seat handover screen (nothing else rendered, clock paused), PIN, turn timer, urgent-event interrupt; a hot-seat game or save opens on the active side's handover. Umpire banner, read-only (no forces panel, unit card, planner or side ROE). Deploy panel. |
| Enemy AI v1 (doc 03 §11, ADR-046) | `wb-ai` depends on `wb-core` and `serde` only. Every second the adapter `wb_sim::ai` builds an `AiPicture` from the AI side's own picture and units, `wb_ai::think` decides, and each action becomes an ordinary order of the side. Strategic (5 min): posture and CAP station count. Operational (30 s): CAP over the air bases nearest the threat with rotation, IADS emitters on alert or doctrine discipline, satellite imaging. Every second: incursion response and retaliation packages after the reaction delay; approvals decided. Each decision is an `AiDecision` event to the AI's side. AI state in the snapshot; replays skip AI orders (the AI issues them again). |

The fighting is heavy in hours 2–5 (the League loses about 100 platforms, the Pact about 90) and almost stops after hour 8: both sides keep CAP up and answer incursions, but the AI v1 starts no offensive of its own (retaliation answers shooters only), so the front settles. ## UI against the references (doc 10 §13)

Screenshots: `P9-setup-players.jpg` (setup, Players step, hot-seat), `P9-handover.jpg`, `P9-umpire-view.jpg` (Command both, umpire view over the Kessan Sea at z6). The setup, handover and deploy screens have no reference screenshot; they follow doc 10 §7–§9 (panel tokens, outlined amber mono buttons, segmented controls). Differences from the references and the guide:

- The top bar wraps onto a second row at 1,536 px wide once the side toggle and umpire switch are in it (`ROE`, `REPORTS` … move down). Not in any reference; a compact layout is left for P12 polish.
- Umpire view: side A's units purple and side B's blue as doc 10 §8 asks; the territory tint keeps side A as own. Weapons of both sides use the own (amber, cyan) styles; doc 10 says nothing about the other side's weapons in the umpire view.
- The side toggle shows the first nation of each coalition (`ALD | VSM`); a coalition of several nations is named in the tooltip.
- The coalition builder assigns nations with A / B / — buttons, not by dragging (doc 03 §2 says "drag").
- Flags are drawn from content colours, as nation data (doc 10 §8 "generated flag"), not tokens.

## Decisions and deviations (please confirm)

- **ADR-046 (Proposed)** records: rejection text identical for unknown and other-side ids with a typed cause (the owner's choice); the setup builder and its fallback; deploy-yourself scope (bases and stationing only, the owner's choice); the AI boundary and its replay rule; the reaction time as doctrine time plus the difficulty's delay. ADR-044 stays Proposed for the owner to accept.
- **Reaction time.** Doc 03 §11 gives difficulty delays (60–120 / 30 / 10 s) and content gives each doctrine a `reaction_time_s`; the AI waits for their sum. The acceptance band is applied to that sum, counted from when the AI's own picture shows the incursion.
- **Default loadouts** for garrisoned and deployed aircraft and ships come from a table of preferred roles by platform category (generic, rule 3), not from content. A `default_loadout` field per platform would move this into data.
- **G16** is an integration test, not a `wb golden` file (it needs view and switch calls between ticks).
- **Group names** now carry the nation id (`ALD Arlen 1`): coalition partners field the same types.
- **The game opens on the menu**, not straight into the quick start; the hosted build's QUICK START is the test war.

## Known gaps

- **Content flags close to real flags (rule 6).** Aldmark's flag (dark blue, yellow Nordic cross) is very close to a real national flag; Vessmar's red, white and blue horizontal bands and Kaldoria's black, gold and red vertical bands also resemble real ones. Content from PF, not changed here; please review `content/nations/`.
- AI v1 does not move IADS sites (sites stay where the garrison put them; the doctrine's `iads_layers` is carried but unused), does not use `missile_reserve_fraction` or `cap_aircraft` (flight sizes come from the garrison), and plans no multi-phase operations (P12).
- Approvals for AI sides are decided at once, without the reaction delay.
- Unplaced forces: Vessmar's 10 `rw-5` helicopters find no room; raising a Vessmar base's capacity or adding a heliport would place them.
- Hot-seat relies on the players not peeking: the client holds both human sides' events (ADR-044).
- Carried over: P8's known gaps.
